Professional fees

Being AMFI (SEBI) registered Mutual Funds Distributor and Insurance Advisor, we don't charge any professional fees to clients. Our revenue comes directly from the respective Fund House or Insurance Company.

Product-level charges, commissions, expense ratios, premiums, taxes and other costs may apply according to the provider and product documents. Please review all relevant disclosures before making a decision.
