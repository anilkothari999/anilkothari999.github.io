Stock Broking

Access to securities markets through a registered broker and their platform.

Potential benefits

Market access, research tools and transaction reporting may be available depending on the broker.

Equity and derivative trading can cause substantial losses. Check broker registration, charges and product risks.

LAMF (Loan Against Mutual Funds)

A loan secured against eligible mutual fund units, subject to lender approval and terms.

Potential benefits

May provide liquidity without immediately redeeming pledged units.

Interest, margin calls, collateral valuation and forced-sale risks apply. Compare borrowing costs and alternatives.
